Video News Script: ' Scotland Wants to Extend International Student Visas—But Can They Really Stay?’ 

Opening Scene: Introducing the topic 

Voiceover: Introduction to the PSW policy– PSW visa, known as post study work visa., is a UK government policy that allows eligible international students to stay in the UK after graduation to work or seek employment.  

In 2025, the Scottish government formally proposed the ‘Scottish Graduate Visa’, this visa aimed at allowing international graduates to stay in Scotland for up to two years. The new visa would be granted for up to two years, acting as a bridge between the Study and Graduate visas and the Skilled Worker Visa. Scotland First Minister John Swinney highlighted the visa’s role in addressing Scotland’s population challenges and supporting economic growth. However, similar proposals were put forward in 2016 and 2024 but were both rejected by the UK government on the grounds of maintaining a nationally unified immigration policy. 

Background Briefing: Policy and Employment Environment 

Voiceover: The PSW visa was introduced in 2008 to allow international graduates to stay and work in the UK for two years, enhancing the attractiveness of the UK as a study destination. Four years after the policy was introduced, the visa was cancelled due to immigration control. Following this change, the number of Indian students in the UK has dropped by nearly 60%. Until 2020, the UK government reinstated this visa policy again. The number of international students applying for PSW visa is rising every year. However, many international students still face challenges when applying for a visa and finding a job.
